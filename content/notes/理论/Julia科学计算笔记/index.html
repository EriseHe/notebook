Exercise 33.2.2

You’re asked to verify eq. (33.30):

Use the divergence theorem in index notation (eqs. (33.28)–(33.29)):

[ _V _i F^{ij},dV = _S F^{ij},dA_i.]

[ _V _i_m(T{im}x’j x’^k),dV = _S _m(T{im}x’j x’^k),dA_i,]
